    Selection

    Draw around an area to select it; tap the canvas to deselect. While something is selected, drawing, erasing, blending and filling only change the inside. Use Transform to move, resize or rotate it.

    Sound

    MP3, WAV, M4A or OGG. Sound plays along when you press Play (in Loop and Once modes). "Starts at" is when a sound begins, counted from frame 1. Recording plays your animation from the current frame so you can speak in time with it.

    Puppet parts

    In-betweens

    Pose the puppet on two frames: every frame between them moves by itself. Need more in-betweens? Add frames after this one:

    3

    How it works

    1. Draw your character's parts (head, body, arms…) a little apart from each other.
    2. Circle one part with Select and tap Make a part. Repeat for every part.
    3. With the Rig tool, choose Set up joints: tap a part, drag its dot to the joint (like the shoulder), tap Attach to… and then tap the part it hangs from.
    4. Choose Pose. On a frame, drag parts to bend them. Go to a later frame and pose again: the frames between move smoothly by themselves (◆ marks your poses).
    5. To change a part's drawing everywhere, select it and tap Edit drawing.

    Insert image

    Use PNG or JPG pictures. You can also drag pictures onto the canvas, or paste one (Ctrl+V). Tip: to trace over a picture, lower its layer's opacity in Layers.

    View

    Touch

    When a stylus is detected, finger drawing turns off automatically, so your palm won't leave marks. Fingers can still zoom, rotate and undo.

    Drawing helpers

    Draw a line, circle, ellipse, rectangle or triangle, then keep your pen down and hold still for a moment. It snaps into a clean shape.

    To fill an area, drag the color circle from the top bar onto it. A higher value lets the fill spread into similar colors too.

    Gestures

    • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, rotate and move the canvas
    • Tap with two fingers to undo
    • Tap with three fingers to redo
    • Touch and hold with one finger for the quick wheel (or pick a color: see Customize quick wheel)
    • Right-click or press Q for the quick wheel
    • Alt + click (Option + click on Mac) to pick a color
    • Mouse wheel to zoom, Shift + wheel to rotate
    • Right-click and drag to move the canvas
