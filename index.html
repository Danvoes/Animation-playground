    Selection

    Draw around an area to select it; tap the canvas to deselect. While something is selected, drawing, erasing, blending and filling only change the inside. Use Transform to move, resize or rotate it.

    Project

    Your work is saved in this browser automatically.

    View

    Touch

    When a stylus is detected, finger drawing turns off automatically, so your palm won't leave marks. Fingers can still zoom, rotate and undo.

    Drawing helpers

    Draw a line, circle, ellipse, rectangle or triangle, then keep your pen down and hold still for a moment. It snaps into a clean shape.

    To fill an area, drag the color circle from the top bar onto it. A higher value lets the fill spread into similar colors too.

    Gestures

    • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, rotate and move the canvas
    • Tap with two fingers to undo
    • Tap with three fingers to redo
    • Touch and hold with a finger to pick a color (when finger drawing is off)
    • Alt + click (Option + click on Mac) to pick a color
    • Mouse wheel to zoom, Shift + wheel to rotate
    • Right-click and drag to move the canvas
